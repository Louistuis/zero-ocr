Runs on any Apple silicon Mac. Shines on a newer one.

Minimum
  • Apple M1Any Mac with Apple silicon. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • macOS 14 SonomaWith the system material instead of Liquid Glass.
  • 8 GB memoryThe model uses about 1.2 GB while it reads, and nothing when it sleeps.
  • 1 GB free spaceThe app is 32 MB. The model is 837 MB, downloaded once.
Recommended
  • Apple M2 or laterAbout 80 tokens a second on an M3 MacBook Air. Pro and Max chips are faster still.
  • macOS 26 TahoeFor the Liquid Glass card. Translate needs macOS 15 or later.
  • 16 GB memoryRoom to keep the model loaded next to your other apps for instant reads.
  • 2 GB free spaceSpace for the model plus a long History of reads with their images.

An internet connection is needed once, to download the model. After that it works offline.

Opening it the first time

Zero OCR is open source and not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS asks once. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Zero OCR.app"
