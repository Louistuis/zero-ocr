In the app the card is SwiftUI Liquid Glass and the neon is a Metal shader. This is the same idea in CSS.

Simple

One shortcut to remember

⇧⌘2 reads any area, or a whole window if you click. Everything else is a button on the card that appears.

Smart

More than plain text

Tables paste as real grids, Markdown and LaTeX show formatted. Links, dates, addresses and QR codes become buttons. Translate, speak, pin, and search everything you read.

Private

Nothing leaves your Mac

The model runs on your Mac's GPU at about 80 tokens a second, then sleeps to free its memory. No account, no upload. It works offline.

Opening it the first time

Zero OCR is open source and not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS asks once. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Zero OCR.app"
