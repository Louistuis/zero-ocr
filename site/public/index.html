Harbour log, winter

The lighthouse keeper logged every passing ship in a leather notebook. On the night of 14 March 1987 he recorded a freighter named Halvard, bound for Rotterdam with 2,340 tonnes of timber.

The weather was poor: wind from the northwest at 38 knots, visibility under two nautical miles. He noted that the vessel's starboard light flickered twice before vanishing behind the headland.

Native

Everywhere you need it

A shortcut for the screen, Quick Actions for images and PDFs in Finder, Services in every app, and zocr in Terminal.

Private

Nothing leaves your Mac

The model runs on your Mac's GPU. No account, no upload, no analytics. It works offline.

Fast

Ready before you let go

The model wakes while you select and reads at about 80 tokens a second, then sleeps to free its memory.

Opening it the first time

Zero OCR is open source and not signed with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS asks once. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Zero OCR.app"
